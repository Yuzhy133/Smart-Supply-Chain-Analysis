## Topic of the project：Dataco Supply Chain Data Analysis

We plan to explore the relationships among features such as orders, products, customers, and transportation based on the supply chain dataset provided by Dataco. Through data cleaning, visualization, analysis, and modeling, we aim to identify issues related to delays, inventory, and costs in the supply chain.
